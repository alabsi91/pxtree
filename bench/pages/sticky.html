Overview

Brightpath plans delivery routes for regional bakeries and grocers. Drivers get a route on their phone the evening before, and dispatchers see every van on one map.

Most customers cut their fuel bill by a fifth in the first quarter, mostly by removing the second trip to the same neighborhood.

Routes respect delivery windows, van capacity and driver breaks. When an order changes after the plan is published, only the affected stops move.

Meet the team

We are eleven people in Leeds, most of us former dispatchers and drivers. Every new hire spends their first week riding along on real routes.

FAQ

Do drivers need a special phone? No, any Android or iPhone from the last five years works.

Can we import orders? Yes, from CSV or directly from Shopify, WooCommerce and most wholesale tools.

Is there a free trial? Thirty days on up to five vans, no card needed.
