A practical guide to data retention for small companies

How long you may keep customer records, what to delete first, and how to write a retention policy your team will actually follow.

Start with an inventory

List every place customer data lives: your CRM, your billing tool, shared drives, support inboxes and the spreadsheet someone exported two years ago. You cannot set a retention period for data you do not know you have.

Tip: ask each team lead for their five most used tools. That covers most of it.

Set a period per category

Invoices usually have a legal minimum, often seven to ten years. Marketing contacts do not. Support conversations sit in between. Write the period next to each category and the reason for it.

Automate the deletion

A policy nobody runs is a liability. Most tools can delete or anonymize records after a set period. Where they cannot, put a recurring reminder on a named person's calendar.

Datenschutzgrundverordnung

The German name of the GDPR, which you will see on most EU templates.
